1.Information We Collect

1.1 Account information

When you register for a Tafuta account, we collect:

  • Full name and, optionally, a nickname
  • Phone number (used as your primary login identifier and for one-time password (OTP) verification)
  • Email address (optional if you register by phone; required if you sign in with Google)
  • Password (stored only as a salted hash — we never store or can retrieve your plaintext password), where applicable
  • Google account identifier and profile info (name, email, profile photo) if you choose "Sign in with Google"
  • Language preference and, if you provide one, a profile photo
  • Marketing preferences — whether you've opted in to SMS or email marketing
  • The version and timestamp of the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy you accepted

1.2 Business information

If you create or manage a business listing, we collect information about that business, including:

  • Business name, category, region, description, contact details, and other listing/website content you enter (stored as structured, multi-language content)
  • Your chosen tafuta.ke subdomain, if you purchase website hosting
  • Logos, banners, profile images, and gallery photos you upload (we process these — cropping, resizing, format conversion — to generate the versions displayed on the Service)
  • Which staff accounts have owner, admin, or employee access to the listing
  • A version history of content changes, so listings can be reviewed or restored by our moderation team

1.3 Payment information

If you purchase a paid service (such as website hosting, ads, search promotion, or an image gallery upgrade), payment is processed by Pesapal, a licensed third-party payment service provider. Tafuta does not collect or store your card, mobile money PIN, or bank account details — these are entered directly with Pesapal. We retain records of the transaction itself: amount, VAT charged, currency (KES), service purchased, payment status, and a receipt number, for accounting and tax-compliance purposes.

1.4 Authentication & security logs

To protect your account and the Service, we log authentication and administrative events — such as login attempts, OTP requests, password resets, and account status changes — together with the IP address, device/browser information, and timestamp associated with each event. Administrative actions taken on your account or listing (e.g., approval, suspension, content rollback) are similarly logged with the acting admin, reason, and timestamp.

1.5 Communications

We use VintEx to deliver SMS messages (OTP codes, payment confirmations, service reminders) and Mailgun to deliver transactional and, where you've opted in, marketing email. These providers process the phone number or email address and message content needed to deliver your message.

1.6 Information collected automatically

When you use the Service, we and our infrastructure providers automatically collect technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring pages, and general usage/log data (pages viewed, searches run, errors encountered). This helps us secure the Service, diagnose problems, and understand how it's used. Tafuta is installable as a Progressive Web App (PWA); if installed, some app assets are cached on your device for offline/faster access (see Section 6).

2.How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the Service — create and manage your account, publish and display business listings and websites, process payments and issue VAT receipts, and deliver OTP codes and notifications.
  • To review and moderate listings — new and edited business listings go through an approval process before they appear publicly; we may use submitted content and contact details to verify a business is genuine.
  • To secure the Service — detect and prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and to enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To communicate with you — service notices (payment receipts, expiry reminders, account/security alerts) and, only if you've opted in, marketing messages by SMS or email.
  • To comply with legal and tax obligations — including Kenyan VAT record-keeping requirements for transactions and receipts.
  • To improve the Service — understand usage patterns and troubleshoot issues, generally using aggregated or de-identified data.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data to serve third-party behavioral advertising.

3.Legal Basis for Processing

Tafuta is operated by eBiashara Rahisi Ltd, a company registered in Kenya, and we process personal data in accordance with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 and its regulations. Depending on the activity, we rely on one or more of the following bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to create your account, publish your listing, and process the services you purchase.
  • Legal obligation — such as VAT record-keeping and responding to lawful requests from Kenyan authorities.
  • Legitimate interests — securing the Service, preventing fraud, and improving our product, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent — for marketing SMS/email and for optional information such as a profile photo; you may withdraw consent at any time (see Section 9).

4.Public Nature of Business Listings

Important Tafuta is a public directory. Once a business listing is approved, the business name, category, region, description, contact details, logo, photos, and any published website content are publicly visible to anyone who visits tafuta.ke or the business's tafuta.ke subdomain — this is the core purpose of the Service.

Do not include personal information in business listing content that you do not want to be public. Your Tafuta account details (password, private phone/email used for login, payment history) are not published — only the business content you or your team choose to publish is shown publicly.

6.Cookies, Local Storage & the Installable App

Tafuta uses a small number of essential cookies/local storage entries to keep you signed in (your session token) and to remember your language preference. Because Tafuta can be installed as a Progressive Web App, your browser or device may cache static assets (images, scripts, styles) locally for faster loading and limited offline access — this cached data stays on your device and is controlled through your browser or device settings.

We do not currently use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If that changes, we will update this Policy. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and clear cached app data at any time; doing so may require you to sign in again.

7.How Long We Keep Information

  • Account data is kept while your account is active. If you deactivate or request deletion of your account, we deactivate/delete it from active systems, but may retain limited data where needed to prevent fraud, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or comply with law.
  • Transaction and receipt records (including VAT-related data) are retained for the period required by Kenyan tax law, generally several years after the transaction date.
  • Authentication and audit logs are retained for a limited period sufficient for security investigation and abuse prevention, after which they are deleted or anonymized.
  • Published business listing content may be retained in version history for moderation and dispute-resolution purposes even after an individual edit is superseded.

8.How We Keep Information Safe

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including password hashing, encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), access controls limiting who can view account and payment data, and logging of administrative actions. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your login credentials and OTP codes confidential and notify us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

9.Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, including Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information — you can update most account and listing details directly in your dashboard
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal information, subject to the retention needs described in Section 7
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent (e.g., marketing)
  • Opt out of marketing SMS and email at any time — via your account settings, an unsubscribe link, or by replying STOP to an SMS. You will still receive essential service and security messages.
  • Lodge a complaint with Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) if you believe we have processed your data unlawfully

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13.

10.Children's Privacy

Tafuta is intended for business owners and staff and is not directed at children. You must be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority in your jurisdiction, to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

11.International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers (for example, content delivery and cloud infrastructure providers) may process or route data through servers located outside Kenya. Where this occurs, we take steps to ensure the provider offers an adequate level of data protection, consistent with the requirements of Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

12.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you through the Service or by email/SMS. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect means you accept the revised Policy. We record the version and date of the policy each user has accepted.
